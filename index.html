BLOCKFIRE

Play in your browser with friends, no install needed

Your browser is drawing graphics without your graphics chip, so the game will run at only a few frames per second.
Chrome / Edge: Settings › System › turn on Use graphics acceleration when available, then relaunch.
On a school or work computer this setting may be locked; try Safari (Mac) or another browser.
Play with friends: one person picks HOST and shares the 6-letter join code; everyone else picks JOIN and types it in.
First visit downloads about 70 MB, plus about 50 MB for each map the first time you play it (the rest download in the background while you play). After that it starts straight away.
Needs a keyboard and mouse and an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on a computer. Website players play with other website players.
